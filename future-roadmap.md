# Future Roadmap (Parked Ideas)

To prevent scope creep and ensure we ship v1, the following ambitious features and concepts have been parked for future iterations (v2.0+).

## Features
1. **Future Echo (Timeline Simulator)**
   - Draggable timeline showing the room evolving over 5+ years (e.g., adding a crib, converting to a home office).
   - Requires multiple 3D asset states and complex morphing logic.

2. **Room Consciousness (Mouse-Attention Tracking)**
   - The room learns from cursor movements and anticipates user intent, reacting pseudo-intelligently before a click happens.

3. **Advanced Ambient Systems**
   - *Emotional Weather*: Lighting and particle effects that simulate moods.
   - *Space Pulse*: Rhythmic breathing of the room.
   - *Furniture Conversations*: Objects interacting or acknowledging each other.
   - *(Note: For v1, these are merged into a single, simple "room breathes" ambient hover effect).*

4. **Cinematic WebGL Preloader**
   - Glowing particles forming a chair before the site loads. Nice to have, but deferred to v1.1.

## Philosophy
Keep the core loop tight: **Birth -> Negotiation -> Life**. Everything else goes here.
